# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and technically constructive
community. Participate in good faith, assume positive intent, and focus
feedback on code and evidence rather than people. Harassment, discrimination,
personal attacks, doxxing, and unwanted contact are not acceptable.

Maintainers may remove comments, close discussions, or restrict participation
when needed to keep the project safe and productive. Report conduct concerns
privately to the maintainer listed in the repository metadata.
